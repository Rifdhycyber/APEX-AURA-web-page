Apexaura Int (Pvt) Ltd

Company introduction

Apexaura (Apexaura Int (Pvt) Ltd) is a Sri Lankan company established in 2014, focused on promoting Sri Lankan herbal food supplements, beauty products, and traditional wellness, emphasizing organic, high‑quality natural ingredients like Moringa, Nutgrass, and Garcinia, with certifications (ISO, GMP) and international reach. Led by Mr. Sheekshana Gunawardhana, they operate from Malabe, Sri Lanka, and aim to provide holistic health through their products and a unique business model for entrepreneurs.

Company overview
